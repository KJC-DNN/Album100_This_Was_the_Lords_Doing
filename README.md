# Album 100 — This Was the Lord's Doing

Eight bilingual songs. This was the Lord’s doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes. Water becomes wine. Nets fill. The wind ceases. Five thousand eat, and twelve baskets remain. Jesus the Healer comes today. The feast day of the Lord is coming. Come, you who are vexed by an unclean spirit. Jesus who died rose on the third day. Gabriel brings the message. Michael contends. The fallen cherub cannot rule. The Spirit gives gifts. Holy ones, bear the fruit. Tamil original plus English cinematic companion. Sense kept. Century album of the book.

## Tracklist

1. This Was the Lord’s Doing — Mark 12:11; John 2; Luke 5; Mark 4; 6; 8; Matthew 14; 17; 21; John 21
2. Jesus the Healer Comes Today — John 4; Matthew 8; Mark 1–2; 5; 7
3. The Feast Day of the Lord Is Coming — John 5; Mark 8; John 9; Luke 14; 17; 22
4. Come, You Who Are Vexed by an Unclean Spirit — Mark 1:23; Matthew 12; 17; Luke 13
5. Jesus Who Died Rose on the Third Day — Mark 5; Luke 7; John 11
6. Gabriel, Michael, and the Fallen Cherub — Luke 1; Jude 9; Ezekiel 28; Isaiah 14; Zechariah 3; Matthew 4
7. The Spirit Gives Gifts — 1 Corinthians 12
8. Holy Ones, Will You Bear the Fruit — Galatians 5:19–24

## Prayer and study
1. Prayer: Read what is written. Study: Mark 12:11 — Cana, the nets, the calm, the five thousand, the walk on the sea, the fig tree, the one hundred and fifty-three fish. It is marvellous in our eyes.
2. Prayer: Let the Healer come. Study: John 4 and Mark 5 — Your son lives. I will, be clean. Speak only a word. Your faith has saved you. Ephphatha.
3. Prayer: Come to the feast. Study: John 5 and John 9 — Bethesda, Siloam, ten lepers, the ear restored. The works of God are revealed.
4. Prayer: Come out. Study: Mark 1:23 and Luke 13 — Be silent. You are loosed. The Holy Spirit sets free.
5. Prayer: Arise. Study: Mark 5; Luke 7; John 11 — Talitha cumi. Young man, arise. Lazarus, come forth. He rose on the third day.
6. Prayer: Worship the Lord only. Study: Jude 9; Ezekiel 28; Matthew 4 — Gabriel brings. Michael contends. Get behind me, Satan. He cannot be like the Most High.
7. Prayer: Ask, and receive. Study: 1 Corinthians 12 — differences of gifts, one Spirit. Wisdom, knowledge, faith, healing, prophecy, discerning, tongues.
8. Prayer: Crucify the desires. Live in the Spirit. Study: Galatians 5:19–24 — love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faith, gentleness, self-control. Put away the works of the flesh.
